    Our Four Sourcing Commitments

    We preserve nature's biological blueprint by adhering to stringent harvesting, genetic authentication, and environmental safeguards.

    Micro-Climate Terroir

    We harvest solely from indigenous micro-climates where soil mineral density and altitude stimulate maximum phytochemical expression naturally.

    DNA Genetic Authentication

    Every botanical lot is authenticated with ITS2 genetic DNA barcoding, ensuring exact species identity with zero adulteration or substitute fillers.

    Supercritical Fluid CO₂

    We completely eliminate petroleum solvents (hexane, ethanol, acetone), extracting delicate monoterpenes with clean, recycled subcritical CO₂.

    Zero Heavy Metal Tolerance

    Inductively Coupled Plasma Mass Spectrometry (ICP-MS) screens every batch for lead, arsenic, cadmium, mercury, and 140+ agricultural pesticides.
