Orders

Payments through Razorpay (the UPI QR code, Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM and cards) confirm by themselves and show under Paid. Payments to your own UPI QR code need you (the site shows it only when Razorpay is not available): before you confirm one, find the UTR and the amount in your bank or UPI app. A UTR typed by a customer is not proof of payment until you see the money arrive. The order number is in the payment note too.
