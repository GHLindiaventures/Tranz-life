Planetary Stewardship

Four Pillars of Ecological Design

How we protect soil vitality, eliminate single-use plastic, and build regenerative prosperity for farming communities.

Commitment 01

Closed-Loop Supercritical Extraction

Traditional botanical manufacturing releases tons of volatile organic solvents (hexane, acetone) into the atmosphere. Our closed-loop system recycles 100% of the food-grade CO₂ fluid continuously, while exhausted botanical plant pulp is converted into nutrient-dense compost for organic agricultural soils.

Commitment 02

Biophotonic Glass & Bio-Fiber Closures

We completely eliminate petroleum blister packs. Formulations are housed in high-density amber pharmaceutical glass that naturally blocks destructive visible light rays (400–700nm). Our outer shipping sleeves are manufactured with FSC-certified 100% post-consumer recycled unbleached kraft paperboard printed with non-toxic algae inks.

Commitment 03

Ethical Wildcrafting & Fair Trade

Over 450 generational harvesting families across the Western Ghats and Nilgiri biosphere partner directly with TranzLife. We provide guaranteed above-market fair-trade wages, harvest limits that preserve rhizome root regeneration, and direct community endowments for rural education and clean water access.

Commitment 04

65% Rooftop Solar Powered Facilities

Our cleanroom encapsulation facility and analytical testing laboratories are equipped with high-efficiency rooftop photovoltaic solar arrays, delivering over 65% of our monthly electrical grid demand and offsetting more than 48 metric tons of grid carbon annually.
