DPDP Act 2023 & Global Data Ethics

Privacy Policy & Data Stewardship

At TranzLife Biosciences, we hold individual privacy to the same exacting standards as our clinical extractions. We collect only what is strictly necessary to formulate and dispatch our botanical products.

Effective: Nov 10, 2026 • Version 2.4 • Fiduciary: TranzLife Biosciences Pvt. Ltd.

Table of Contents

1. Information We Collect 2. How We Use Your Data 3. Statutory Grounds (DPDP) 4. Health Insights & Privacy 5. Third-Party Disclosures 6. Cookies & Telemetry 7. Storage & Encryption 8. Your Statutory Rights 9. Grievance Redressal

1. Information We Collect

TranzLife Biosciences Pvt. Ltd. ("TranzLife", "we", "our", or "us") gathers data directly from you when you navigate our digital platform, subscribe to clinical whitepapers, utilize our botanical product finder, or place an order for our phyto-active formulations.

  • Identity & Contact Data: Full name, verified mobile number, delivery address, billing address, and electronic mail address.
  • Transaction & Order History: SKU purchases, batch allocation numbers, payment receipt identifiers, fulfillment milestones, and customer service exchanges. Note: Payment card details and net-banking credentials are processed directly via PCI-DSS Level 1 compliant gateways (e.g. Razorpay, Stripe) and are never stored on TranzLife servers.
  • Wellness & Consultation Preferences: Voluntary inputs provided in our lifestyle assessment quizzes, wellness consultation inquiries, or customer support dialogues.
  • Technical & Telemetry Data: IP addresses (anonymized at subnet level), browser user-agent tokens, operating system profiles, referring URLs, and interactive navigation performance markers collected via privacy-centric analytics.
Zero Biological Data Policy: TranzLife does not store, sequence, or commercialize genetic, biometric, or physiological specimen information on this consumer website. Any future precision genetics screening services are managed under separate, dedicated medical ethics protocols.

2. How We Use Your Information

We handle your personal data solely for legitimate, research-driven, and commercial fulfillment objectives:

  • Order Fulfillment & Logistics: Processing payments, batch allocation, temperature-controlled cold-chain shipping, and delivery notifications via SMS/WhatsApp/Email.
  • Customer Care & Formulation Inquiries: Answering scientific questions regarding botanical bioavailability, contraindications with prescription therapies, and batch Certificates of Analysis.
  • Regulatory Quality Traceability: Maintaining statutory batch records in accordance with AYUSH Good Manufacturing Practices (GMP) and ISO 22000:2018 food safety recall protocols.
  • Clinical Whitepapers & Science Updates: Disseminating peer-reviewed botanical research, clinical trial readouts, and company milestones with explicit, granular opt-in consent.
  • Platform Security & Fraud Prevention: Protecting our digital infrastructure against distributed denial-of-service (DDoS) vectors, fraudulent transactions, and unauthorized access attempts.

3. Statutory Grounds for Processing (DPDP Act 2023 & GDPR)

Under the Indian Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023 and the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), every data processing operation conducted by TranzLife is anchored in one of the following legal grounds:

  • Consent (Section 6, DPDP Act 2023 / Article 6(1)(a) GDPR): When you affirmatively subscribe to newsletters or voluntarily input wellness preferences in our interactive tools. You may withdraw consent at any time.
  • Contractual Performance: When processing is indispensable to deliver ordered botanical products to your designated address and provide order confirmations.
  • Statutory Compliance: When retaining financial records, tax invoices (GST), and AYUSH batch traceability logs as mandated under Indian pharmaceutical and commercial legislation.
  • Legitimate Scientific Interest: Aggregated, fully anonymized behavioral metrics utilized to optimize server performance and molecular educational interfaces.

4. Health Insights & Wellness Information

When interacting with the TranzLife Product Finder or our clinical wellness advisors, you may choose to disclose wellness goals (e.g., restorative sleep patterns, metabolic vitality, or joint flexibility).

This data is categorized as Sensitive Wellness Preference Data. It is encrypted in transit using TLS 1.3 and at rest with AES-256 GCM encryption. It is accessible exclusively by authorized clinical support personnel bound by strict non-disclosure obligations. We will never sell, lease, or monetize your wellness data to insurance underwriters, data brokers, or advertisement exchanges.

5. Data Sharing & Third-Party Processors

TranzLife does not trade personal data for commercial profit. We engage trusted third-party service providers (Data Processors) solely to execute operational tasks:

  • Logistics Partners: Blue Dart, Delhivery, and DHL for secure dispatch and last-mile order delivery.
  • Payment Gateways: RBI-licensed, PCI-DSS Level 1 compliant processors for encrypted transaction handling.
  • Transactional Communications: Enterprise messaging gateways (Twilio / Gupshup) for critical OTP generation, delivery updates, and customer support.
  • Analytical Infrastructure: Self-hosted or privacy-first telemetry platforms configured with IP anonymization and zero cross-site tracker sharing.
  • Statutory Authorities: Law enforcement agencies or courts only when legally compelled under valid Indian judicial warrants or statutory mandates.

6. Cookies & Telemetry Technologies

Our digital platform utilizes modern, non-invasive cookies to ensure website reliability and user session continuity:

  • Essential Cookies: Preserving items in your shopping bag (`tl_bag_state`) and maintaining security CSRF tokens. These cannot be disabled as the site cannot function without them.
  • Performance & Diagnostics: Anonymized metrics on page load latency, 3D WebGL compound render performance, and 60 FPS frame continuity.
  • Preference Storage: Remembering whether you prefer dark mode or specific batch metric units.

You can configure your browser to reject all third-party cookies or alert you when cookies are placed. Our website does not utilize invasive third-party cross-site advertising trackers.

7. Storage, Security & Data Retention

We implement state-of-the-art administrative, technical, and physical safeguards:

  • End-to-End Encryption: All client-to-server traffic is enforced over HTTPS with TLS 1.3 protocol and HSTS headers.
  • Database Encryption: Persistent records are shielded using AES-256 encryption at rest within tier-4 Indian data centers located in Bengaluru and Mumbai.
  • Access Control: Strict Principle of Least Privilege (PoLP) and multi-factor authentication (MFA) across all staff accounts.
  • Retention Schedule: Order records and tax invoices are retained for 7 years to comply with Indian GST and commercial regulations. Inactive wellness quiz responses are permanently purged after 18 months.

8. Your Statutory Rights

In accordance with the DPDP Act 2023, GDPR, and consumer protection mandates, you possess the following enforceable rights:

  • Right to Access & Summary: Request an itemized disclosure of the personal data TranzLife holds regarding your identity.
  • Right to Correction & Erasure: Rectify inaccurate address records or request the deletion of obsolete data not mandated by statutory tax retention.
  • Right of Grievance Redressal: Direct escalation to our designated Data Protection Officer for prompt resolution of privacy concerns.
  • Right to Nominate: Nominate another individual to exercise your privacy rights in the event of death or incapacity.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: Unsubscribe from research digests and marketing communications at any time via the one-click link included in every email.
