Operational Architecture

The Five Pillars of TranzLife Quality

How we protect botanical active compounds from degradation, cross-contamination, and environmental oxidation.

Pillar 01

ISO 7 Cleanroom Suites

Air in our production facilities passes through multi-stage 0.3-micron HEPA filtration with 30+ air changes per hour. Positive atmospheric pressure ensures ambient outdoor particles can never enter.

Pillar 02

Sub-32°C Cold Extraction

High temperatures destroy volatile plant monoterpenes. We utilize closed-loop supercritical CO₂ systems operating strictly below 32°C, capturing pure botanical profiles without heat alteration.

Pillar 03

High-Resolution Chromatography

In-house High-Performance Liquid Chromatography (HPLC) and Gas Chromatography-Mass Spectrometry (GC-MS) quantify the precise molecular milligram concentrations of active biomarkers in every lot.

Pillar 04

Double-Quarantine Protocol

No incoming raw material enters the active production line until two independent NABL-accredited third-party laboratories issue matching COAs for genetic identity, purity, and safety.

Pillar 05

Hermetic Nitrogen Sealing

Immediately prior to cap crimping, bottles undergo sterile nitrogen displacement. Removing headspace oxygen prevents lipid oxidation and guarantees full potency across the 24-month shelf life.

Process Rigor

The Cleanroom Manufacturing Sequence

A linear closed-loop production sequence that eliminates human cross-contamination from raw herb to finished unit.

STEP 01

Raw Herb Quarantine

Incoming botanical lots are isolated in humidity-controlled bays for DNA barcoding and heavy metal assay verification.

STEP 02

Cryo Micronization

Botanicals are flash-frozen to -40°C and milled into microscopic particles, fracturing cell walls without heat friction.

STEP 03

Supercritical CO₂

Subcritical solvent-free fluid extraction isolates pure monoterpenes and lipophilic actives with zero chemical traces.

STEP 04

Phytosome Bi-Layer

Actives are micro-encapsulated into sunflower phosphatidylcholine complexes inside ISO 7 positive pressure suites.

STEP 05

Nitrogen Pack & Release

Bottled in UV-blocking glass with nitrogen displacement, followed by secondary quarantine before logistics dispatch.
