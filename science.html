The Cellular Delivery Platform

Solving The Botanical Absorption Bottleneck

Raw botanicals exhibit notoriously poor human bioavailability due to gastric acid degradation, large molecular weights, and rapid hepatic clearance. TranzLife's multi-tier pharmaceutical platform overcomes these barriers.

Pillar 01 / Thermodynamics

Supercritical CO₂ Fluid Extraction

By tuning temperature to exactly 31.1°C and pressure above 73.8 bar, carbon dioxide enters a supercritical state. It penetrates plant cellular matrices like a gas while dissolving precious active lipids like a solvent, leaving zero chemical residues.

31.1°C Critical Temp 73.8 Bar Controlled Zero Hexane / Ethanol
Pillar 02 / Cellular Kinetics

Phytosome & Liposomal Bi-Layers

Active polyphenol compounds are molecularly bonded to non-GMO sunflower phosphatidylcholine. This bio-compatible lipid envelope shields fragile molecules through the corrosive stomach environment and merges directly with small intestinal enterocytes.

Phosphatidylcholine Bi-Layer Gastric Acid Resistant Direct Enterocyte Transit
Pillar 03 / Analytics

HPLC Quantitative Standardization

Unlike crude extracts that vary with seasonal weather, our High-Performance Liquid Chromatography (HPLC) assays fingerprint and quantify exact active compound fractions (e.g. 80% Silymarin, 99.2% 1,8-Cineole, 95% Curcuminoids) in every single batch.

HPLC / LC-MS Fingerprinting ±1.5% Batch Reproducibility Validated Reference Standards
Pillar 04 / Synergy

Entourage Bio-Architectures

Single isolated molecules frequently suffer from rapid enzymatic clearance. TranzLife designs multi-terpene botanical architectures where secondary plant flavonoids protect primary active ligands, prolonging circulating plasma half-life up to 8.5 hours.

Full-Spectrum Terpenoids Receptor Co-Activation Prolonged Plasma Half-Life
